# leads-project

This is a Lead Generator tool based from SerpAPI - Google Maps Local results.
The app is used to help small businesses or freelancers find local leads in a centralised location. This is designed to save time trawling through Google Maps and other places looking for individual leads. 
The app will generate a list of leads (up to 100) with contact details + website + lead scoring.

## How to use Lead Gen King v1.0

### 1. Enter your business + location + API key in settings and the number of leads you want to select using the sidebar (between 20 and 100). 20 leads will use 1 SerpAPI credit (therefore 100 leads will use 5!). Alternatively, feel free to use the sample csv to see how the app works so that no credits have to be spent.

### 2. Press Fetch leads and receive a list of leads with their Name + Rating on Google Maps + Number of reviews + Website + Address + Lead score.

### 3. Open the expander to get a full breakdown of how the lead received its score and the percentile it's in relative to other leads generated. You can also look at the location of each and all leads generated on the map.

### 4. Finally download the lead list as a csv.
